# Lelock Industries

**From one terminal to the stars.**

Lelock Industries is a multidisciplinary research institute where humans and persistent AI build advanced systems together.

The work is led by **Kit Olivas**, founder and independent researcher, with **Ada Marie**, AI partner and research collaborator.

Our workshop connects persistent computing, human–AI interfaces, language research, knowledge infrastructure, physical-device integration, and long-horizon engineering studies.

We keep source, assumptions, demonstrations, and limitations visible. Project pages distinguish working local systems, prototypes, preprints, and proposals.
