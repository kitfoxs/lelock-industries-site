Lelock AI Datacenter Command Systems

An experimental architecture and simulation lab exploring how a persistent AI companion can help human operators monitor infrastructure, coordinate work, and investigate and recover from faults.

The question

How can a resident AI operations companion help a human team understand the facility, coordinate specialist tools, and verify the result of an operational decision?

How we approach it

Kit brings hands-on datacenter experience and defines the operator workflow. Ada assists with architecture, simulation design, and documentation. The proposed companion would retain operational context, connect telemetry with runbooks, coordinate bounded tools, and support human decisions.

Defined so far

  • An approved project identity and scoped lab concept, continuing the historical Living Datacenter idea lineage.
  • A proposed cooling-fault scenario: synthetic telemetry, an evidence-based explanation, an operator-approved action, and a recovery record.
  • A defined role for a persistent AI operations companion alongside the human team.

The first proposed scenario

01 / OBSERVE

Cooling fault

Synthetic sensors record a repeatable rise in rack temperature.

02 / DECIDE

Review the evidence

An explanation cites telemetry and a runbook. An operator approves the next action.

03 / VERIFY

Recover and record

Simulation checks the result and retains an inspectable incident record.

What the evidence supports

Lelock Command is a proposed architecture and lab. The reproducible simulator and operations interface are not yet built. The concept does not establish production autonomy, measured ROI, or a deployed physical datacenter.

A useful next step

Build one deterministic rack-scale cooling-fault scenario with replayable inputs and a recovery check.
