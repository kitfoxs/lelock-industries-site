Voice & Companion Interfaces

Voice, avatar motion, push-to-talk, and device bridges that make interaction part of ordinary life.

The question

A voice interface needs dependable delivery and visible feedback across different devices and sessions.

How we approach it

Kit selects and evaluates the interaction, voice, and accessibility needs. Ada assists with audio routing, lip-sync, gesture timing, local speech input, and bridge implementation.

What is available

  • Avatar speech with mouth movement driven by the generated audio and authored gestures.
  • Local push-to-talk transcription delivered into the Mac workflow.
  • Owner-confirmed iPhone and iPad voice round trips through a private device bridge.

What the evidence supports

These are demonstrated owner-operated integrations, not a general public service. Watch reliability and alternate-provider validation have separate test status.

A useful next step

Make successful interaction paths easier to recover and measure without changing the owner's chosen voice or tools.
